Questions

Do tenants need to download an app?

No. Tenants keep paying through M-Pesa or their bank exactly as they do today. The tenant portal is a simple web link that works on any phone.

Do you hold my rent money?

Never. Payments go straight into your own Paybill, Till or bank account. Nyumba only reads the transaction data to reconcile it.

What happens when a tenant uses the wrong account number?

Our matching uses the phone number, amount, name and payment history to suggest the right unit. Anything uncertain lands in a short review queue — one tap to confirm.

Can I import my existing records?

Yes. Upload a spreadsheet of units, tenants, rents and balances and we will map the columns for you. Our team can also do it for you on Growth and Enterprise.

What if I want to leave?

Export all your data to Excel at any time. No contracts on monthly plans, no exit fees.
